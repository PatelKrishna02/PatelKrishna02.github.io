Case studies

AutoAidPlus: a mobile-first website for a car repair service

Freelance, May to Nov 2025. WordPress, Elementor, SEO. Live in production.

Problem
Customers needed a fast way to ask for a repair. The client rarely opens email and does most of his communication on WhatsApp, and he did not want a complex booking system.
My role
Designer and developer, working directly with the client from requirements to launch.
What I did
Designed a short request form that opens a WhatsApp conversation with the customer's details pre-filled, so the customer and the business can talk straight away. I chose WhatsApp over email because it is the channel the client actually checks. I built the site in WordPress with Elementor, using a mobile-first layout, and set up SEO.
Result
The site is live, and customers can reach the business in two steps. I kept improving usability after launch based on client and customer feedback.

Visit autoaidplus.com.au

PU Screen Generator: an internal tool for engineers

Nepean Rubber & Plastics Company, Feb 2025 to Jan 2026. Python, Flask, JavaScript.

Problem
Engineers spent about 45 minutes drafting each screen diagram by hand, and calculation errors kept recurring.
My role
Designed, built and deployed the tool, and supported the team during rollout.
What I did
Gathered requirements from the engineers, then designed a structured form for screen size, strip placement and other options, with input validation. The tool calculates the layout, draws the diagram and exports a production-ready PDF. I refined it based on how they used it day to day.
Result
Drafting dropped from about 45 minutes to under 5, and the recurring errors stopped.

Booking Travel with AI: a full-stack flight booking platform

Western Sydney University capstone, team of four, Aug to Oct 2025. React, Django, PostgreSQL, Claude API, Stripe. Screens from the Iteration 2 client presentation.

Our client wanted a flight booking experience where users could search, get AI help and pay in one place.

What I did
Designed and built the account area: log in and sign up, an editable profile, and booking management with current and past trips. Worked across two Agile iterations with three teammates, alongside flight search from the Amadeus test API and Stripe sandbox payments.

Woven Wire Weight Calculator: fast quoting for sales

Nepean Rubber & Plastics Company. JavaScript, HTML, CSS.

Problem
Estimating woven wire weight needs several inputs, such as aperture, wire diameter and screen size. Staff needed to do it quickly while preparing quotes, and doing it by hand was repetitive and easy to get wrong.
What I did
Designed a clean form with four inputs (aperture, wire diameter, width and length) and an instant result in kilograms, rounded to two decimal places. Added input validation, and a calculation history beside the form so users can look back over earlier results during a session. The history clears when the page is closed. It runs in the browser with nothing to install.
Result
It is live and in active daily use.

About

I'm Krishna Patel, a junior UI/UX and web designer and front-end developer based in Australia. I like taking a messy, real-world problem and turning it into something simple that people can use without needing instructions.

My career started on an IT help desk at a television broadcasting company in Tanzania. For more than two years I helped non-technical staff with everyday computer problems. That taught me how quickly people get frustrated with confusing software, and how much a clear explanation matters. I still design with that person in mind.

I then studied in Nairobi and in Sydney, finishing a Bachelor of Information and Communication Technology at Western Sydney University in 2025 with 4 High Distinctions and 10 Distinctions. My major was Cyber Security and Ethical Hacking, which gave me a habit of thinking about how things can go wrong. You can see it in the input checks I build into my forms.

Since 2025 I have been designing and building real tools for real users: internal tools for engineers at a manufacturing company, a website for a mobile repair business, and a team project with an AI assistant built on the Claude API. I use Figma to design, and HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React to build.

What I bring
